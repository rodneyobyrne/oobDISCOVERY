# Varetto clinician outreach

Use two messages. Send the first with the original persona study attached. Create a different single-use account invitation for each recipient, then send the second message individually from `admin@oobcreative.com`.

The unique account link creates private Varetto Discovery access. The questionnaire has a separate stable URL. Do not reuse or forward an invitation link.

## Email 1 — context and expectation

**Subject:** Varetto persona study and our next Discovery step

Hi Stephanie and Amy,

Attached is the persona study completed when we initially launched Varetto Recovery. I believe the four profiles are a strong starting point—not finished answers—for the addition of therapy services.

I’d like each of you to help us decide what still fits, what should be adjusted or combined, what should be eliminated, and which therapy audiences are missing. Your clinical perspective will help us separate an enduring audience pattern from a diagnosis, demographic, occupation, or temporary help-seeking state.

I’ve created an oobCREATIVE Discovery process so we can keep learning about Varetto’s goals, audience, language, and reach over time. Shortly after this email, each of you will receive a separate message from `admin@oobcreative.com` with your own private account invitation and the questionnaire link.

Please plan for **30–45 minutes** for one persona worksheet. You may complete more than one persona if it is useful; each additional worksheet will take more time. The draft saves for 14 days in the same browser, so you do not have to finish in one sitting.

Please complete your first response independently. I want to preserve the differences in how each of you sees the audience before we compare and reconcile the patterns together.

Thank you,

Rodney

## Email 2 — individual invitation

**Subject:** Your private Varetto Discovery invitation

Hi [Stephanie/Amy],

This is your individual invitation to the Varetto Recovery Discovery process.

1. **Create your private account:** [PASTE UNIQUE INVITATION URL]
2. Check your email and use the verification link to finish activating the account.
3. **Open the persona questionnaire:** https://discovery.oobcreative.com/clinician/

Please plan for **30–45 minutes** for one persona. Start by choosing one of the four personas from the original study, or create a new therapy audience if none fits. The original persona data will remain visible while you respond, but your changes are recorded separately.

Use the microphone if that makes the process easier. Speak naturally and do not edit yourself into polished marketing language. Uncertainty, contradiction, word choice, and small nuances are useful; they help us distinguish audience patterns and later craft content that sounds more like Varetto and like you.

If you already use an AI or writing assistant that reflects your voice, it is fine to paste from it after reviewing the response for accuracy. Do not include patient names or any detail that could identify an individual—in this form or in an external AI tool.

Your account link is single-use and intended only for you. Your draft remains saved for 14 days in the same browser.

Thank you,

Rodney

## Admin sequence

1. Send Email 1 to both recipients with the original study attached.
2. In the Discovery results workspace, open **Manage access**.
3. Create a separate Varetto invitation for Stephanie and Amy. Use **Viewer** unless either person should manage client access.
4. Copy each unique link immediately; the complete token is displayed only once.
5. Send Email 2 individually and replace the bracketed name and invitation URL.
6. Ask each recipient to create and verify the account before beginning the questionnaire. The account is what lets the recipient privately review the submitted response afterward.
7. Revoke any unused invitation that was sent to the wrong address or is no longer needed.

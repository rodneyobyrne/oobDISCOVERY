# Therapy Website Discovery Method

## Immediate objective

Collect enough informed source material to define Varetto's therapy offering, identify a small number of priority audience situations, and write website content that helps those people recognize themselves without requiring them to self-diagnose.

This is a website-content interview, not a clinical assessment, market-research study, or complete service-line planning process.

## Discovery sequence

1. **Confirm launch services** — identify what will actually be available and any important limits.
2. **Select priority situations** — choose up to three recognizable moments or recovery situations the website should understand especially well.
3. **Describe lived experience** — capture what is happening, what remains private, what behavior temporarily provides, what the person wants, and why they may hesitate to seek help.
4. **Connect services and conditions** — identify reasonable clinical associations only after the human pattern has been described.
5. **Capture language** — collect phrases people use, recognition needs, language to avoid, and an honest promise about the experience of care.
6. **Analyze downstream** — oobCREATIVE performs the clustering, research, persona development, and content translation after responses are received.

## Why the form limits audience selection

The goal is not to document every person Varetto could potentially serve. Choosing up to three situations forces a useful website-content priority while leaving room for respondents to select an “other” audience when the prompts miss something important.

The selected situations are starting hypotheses, not finished personas. One behavior can serve different emotional functions, and similar experiences can be associated with different clinical conditions.

## Source integrity

Stakeholder responses combine observation, professional judgment, and planning assumptions. Preserve the original submission unchanged. Store any later LLM analysis, research synthesis, or persona development separately and identify it as interpretation.

## Ethical communication boundary

The objective is better recognition and more appropriate inquiry—not maximum lead volume or patient self-diagnosis.

Public messaging should not promise fit, outcomes, or specialty merely because a respondent associates a clinical concern with an audience situation. Final claims must remain consistent with actual services, licensure, scope, capacity, and human approval.
